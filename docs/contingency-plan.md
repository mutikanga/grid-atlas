# Contingency plan: building the demo if UETCL data does not arrive

**Purpose.** The Grid Atlas demo must go ahead on 27 Nov even if some or all of the 15 data requests are late or refused. This plan says what we use instead, for each dataset, and how every screen stays honest about it.

**Rule.** Nothing that is not UETCL data is ever presented as UETCL data. The software enforces this on every page, chart, map and export through a provenance level:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| REAL | Received from UETCL, NCC or another holder. |
| PUBLIC | Published figures or open data (OpenStreetMap, ERA and UETCL annual reports, UBOS). |
| APPROXIMATE | Hand-built from public geometry and typical engineering values. |
| SYNTHETIC | Generated by `engine/synthetic`, shaped to match public totals. |

A result takes the lowest level of anything that went into it. In data manifests, PUBLIC is written `provenance = "open"`.

## Fallback ladder per dataset

| Data (request) | If it arrives | Fallback 1: public | Fallback 2: approximate or synthetic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Network model (2) | Planning case | Lines and substations from OpenStreetMap (power=line / substation, voltage and circuit tags) | Hand-built T12 model from the OSM routes, with typical ACSR parameters (`config/conductors.csv`) and transformer sizes |
| GIS routes (4) | Planning GIS | OSM line geometry | Straight lines between substations |
| NCC monthly reports (5, 7) | NCC Power System Summaries | Published annual and quarterly totals (UETCL annual report, ERA sector statistics); the team already holds the June to September 2026 summaries cited in the paper | Synthetic monthly series scaled to those published totals |
| SCADA (6) | Historian export | none | Standard daily load shapes (`config/profiles/`) scaled to monthly energy |
| Kawanda constraint, curtailments (8) | NCC operations | Public statements (press, ERA notices) about 2026 curtailments | A scenario: the constraint modelled from transformer and line ratings alone, labelled "scenario" |
| Tariff, deemed energy (9) | Commercial | ERA-published bulk supply tariff and deemed-energy figures | none needed |
| Projects, committed loads (3, 10) | Planning | Grid Development Plan and the Mission 300 Compact (published) | Scenario loads at the industrial parks |
| Sites (13) | UIA | UIA park list and OSM industrial landuse | Hand-placed sites at the parks |
| Feeders (14) | UEDCL | none | Not needed: substation totals are enough |
| Open layers (15) | already public | | |

## What the demo then shows
- **The method, working end to end:** map, losses, N-1, Kawanda, sites, dashboard and exports, all on a PUBLIC or APPROXIMATE Central network with the banner visible.
- **The validation story, told honestly.** The 10% gate cannot be tested without NCC metering. The slide says so, and asks for the data. That ask is itself a demo outcome.
- **Public-figure cross-checks:** total Central demand and national losses against published totals, to show the model is in the right range.
